Include your app version and a short description of the problem. Do not send access codes, NFC credentials, family links, or student photos in a public issue or review.

Instructor sign-in

Open Workspace → Shared workspace and enter your individual instructor ID and access code. If you are exploring the demo, switch to the academy workspace first. Ask an academy administrator for access or a replacement code. Students and parents do not create accounts in this app.

Shared records and offline work

Teaching records stay on your iPhone for offline use. Sign in and connect to the internet to sync with the academy. If you see a conflict, review both versions before choosing which to keep. Do not delete the app to troubleshoot unsynced records; contact the academy first.

Instructor hours

Open Today → My hours. Clock-in requires a fresh, precise location within the configured entrance radius at 118 Ferry Street, Jersey City, and an internet connection. Allow location access while using the app and enable Precise Location in iPhone Settings. If the indoor signal is weak, retry near the entrance or a window.

Clock-out works from anywhere online. Hours remain open until you clock out. Ask an administrator to correct a forgotten clock-out or missed shift; corrections retain a reason and history. The app does not track your location in the background.

NFC cards

NFC requires a compatible physical iPhone and tag. Use your access code to set up a blank writable instructor card. A lost card should be revoked in the instructor-card settings or reported to an administrator. You can still sign in with your access code and open students from the roster without NFC.

Class photos and family updates

Open a daily class logbook to add accomplishments, next steps, and photos. Families see only updates an instructor explicitly shares for their child. Family links open in a browser and can be replaced or revoked. Contact the instructor if a link has expired or a record needs correction.

Privacy and data requests

Read the app privacy policy. For access, correction, or deletion requests, contact the academy or visit its data request page.
