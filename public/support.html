Catalyst Watch Support

Catalyst Watch is an iPhone biotech catalyst monitor with Free and Pro access.

Plans

Free access includes a 30-minute delayed signal feed. Catalyst Watch Pro includes the complete real-time feed, Time Sensitive push notifications, and manual source scans. Purchases and renewals are managed by Apple.

Restore Pro

Open Settings, choose View Pro plans, then select Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account that purchased the subscription.

Troubleshooting

If the app reports a connection error, confirm that the phone has internet access and try again. If Pro is active but notifications are not arriving, open iOS Settings, select Catalyst Watch, and confirm that notifications and Time Sensitive Notifications are enabled.

Contact

For help or to report a problem, open an issue on the Catalyst Watch support tracker.
