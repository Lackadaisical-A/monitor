Catalyst Watch Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026

Catalyst Watch provides a free delayed feed and an optional Pro subscription. It has no advertising, analytics SDK, or third-party tracking.

Data the app uses

The app stores its server address and a random installation identifier in Apple UserDefaults, and stores a random installation credential in the iOS Keychain. It sends the installation identifier and a one-way representation of the credential to the Catalyst Watch server to authenticate requests. When notifications are enabled, it also sends the Apple Push Notification service device token and notification authorization state so the server can deliver eligible alerts.

Watchlists and preferences

The Catalyst Watch server stores the companies and tickers you choose to watch, along with your feed mode, alert event filters, and notification preferences. This information is associated with the random installation identifier and is used only to personalize the feed and deliver requested alerts.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. To verify and maintain Pro access, the app sends App Store-signed transaction information to the Catalyst Watch server. The server stores the product identifier, transaction identifiers, App Store environment, verification date, and subscription expiration or revocation state. Catalyst Watch does not receive full payment-card details.

Developer club attendance

The developer-only club check-in tool can collect a consenting member's name, age, phone number or Instagram handle, grade level, and attendance times. A scanned NFC card identifier is immediately transformed into a keyed one-way fingerprint; the raw card identifier is not retained. Member profile fields are encrypted before they are stored by the Catalyst Watch server. This tool records club attendance only and does not verify Rutgers affiliation or provide access to Rutgers systems.

Who receives data

Installation, watchlist, preference, subscription, device-registration, and optional club-attendance data goes to the Catalyst Watch server and to Apple as required for purchases and push-notification delivery. The developer does not sell personal data or share it for advertising or tracking.

Retention and deletion

On-device settings remain until changed or the app is removed. Server-side installation, watchlist, preference, entitlement, and push-registration records are retained while needed to provide the service, prevent unauthorized access, and honor subscription status. Club members may ask the club operator to delete their attendance profile and associated attendance records directly in the app. Other deletion requests can be submitted through the support page.

Security

The production service uses HTTPS, installation credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain, and App Store transactions are cryptographically verified using Apple's signed transaction data. Club card identifiers are retained only as keyed fingerprints, and club member profiles are encrypted at rest. Developer credentials and club encryption keys are not embedded in the app.

Contact

Questions and support requests can be filed through the Catalyst Watch support page.
