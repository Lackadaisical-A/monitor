What a signal means

A new cash buyback must pass the frozen news, liquidity, authorization size, prior return and market reaction rules. Entry is a next-session opening-auction limit order, capped at 1% above the event close; the planned exit is the close of holding session five.

Evidence before expectations

Win rate and average trade use completed trades with recorded entry and exit fills. Paper fills are simulated and omit market impact, some slippage and fees. The data subscription does not create signals or guarantee returns.
