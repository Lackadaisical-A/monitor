Your academy records

Updated November 4, 2026

The public demo uses fictional students. The academy workspace stores student names, class assignments, projects, goals, homework, assessment evidence, instructor names, learning progress, dated class updates, and photos of student work.

Local and shared storage

Records are saved on your iPhone for offline use. When an approved instructor signs in, the app sends the shared workspace to the academy's backend hosted on Render using HTTPS. Workspace history is encrypted in a separate persistent database. Approved instructors can read the shared roster and learning records. Administrators manage access and curriculum changes.

Instructor access and NFC

Instructors receive individual access codes. The server stores code digests, session digests, and access audit events; it does not store readable access codes. A linked instructor NFC card holds a revocable sign-in token. Treat that card like a key: anyone with the token can use it until it is revoked. Student passport identifiers are stored as keyed digests and do not grant instructor access. The app uses device authentication for sensitive teaching actions and card setup.

Class photos and family updates

Instructors can take a photo or select one using Apple’s photo picker. The app reduces image size and removes location metadata. Photos are stored with device protection on the iPhone and encrypted separately on Render. Draft updates and private instructor notes are available only to approved staff. An instructor can explicitly share a student’s accomplishments, next steps, and photos through a private, read-only family link. Anyone with that link can view that student’s shared updates; give it only to the intended parent or guardian. Links expire after one year and instructors can replace or revoke them. Revocation stops future access, but cannot retract content a recipient has already saved. The app does not send these links automatically.

History, exports, and removal

The service retains workspace versions and access audit events for recovery and accountability. Local recovery files and exported backups may also contain student information. JSON backups include class notes and photo references; the photo files remain on the original device and shared service and are not embedded in those exports. Signing out does not delete locally saved teaching records. Store exported files privately. Contact your academy administrator for access, corrections, retention arrangements, or deletion of a record and its historical copies.

No advertising tracking

Academy data is used for app functionality, synchronization, and recovery. The app does not use academy records for advertising or cross-app tracking. It does not automatically send messages to parents or write attendance back to Google Sheets.
